Hi! I’m a first-year PhD student at Universität Göttingen with Prof. Dr. Lisa Beinborn, working on cognitively plausible strategies for sample-efficient modeling in multilingual NLP.

I completed my Master’s degree at Saarland University, where I worked as a student research assistant in the labs of Prof. Dr. Michael Hahn and Prof. Dr. Vera Demberg.

During my Master’s, I worked on the expressivity of transformer and state-space models, mechanistic interpretability of in-context learning, and persistent theoretical limitations of the transformer architecture.

My research interests revolve around sample-efficient learning, multilinguality, and interpretability.

In my PhD, I am tackling three questions:

  1. Which cognitively inspired inductive biases help language models learn from less data?
  2. Are these biases language-agnostic, or do they need to be tailored to specific languages?
  3. How can we build these biases into model architectures in a way that stays interpretable?
